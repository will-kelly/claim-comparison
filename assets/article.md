# Why review time is the hidden cost in content operations

Every content team knows the drafting part. Fewer teams measure what happens
after a draft is done: the days it waits for an editor, the back-and-forth
over sources, and the final sign-off.

We built Lumen to shorten that wait. Lumen flags statements in a draft that
lack a cited source, so editors spend their time on judgment calls instead of
hunting for missing links.

## What the pilot showed

We ran a pilot with four B2B technology content teams in 2026. Across 212
drafts, median review time fell from 3.0 days to 1.8 days. Lumen will cut your
review time by 40%.

## How it fits your workflow

Lumen works inside Google Docs and Notion. It suggests edits; a human editor
approves every change, and nothing goes live without that approval.

## Who it is for

Lumen is designed for any content team, from solo creators to global
enterprises. If you publish, Lumen will make you faster.
